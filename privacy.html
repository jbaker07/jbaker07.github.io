Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy covers the Baker Supply keyword research app, operated by Baker Supply Works for its own internal use.

What the app accesses

With the operator's consent through Google sign-in, the app uses the Google Ads API scope to read aggregate keyword planning data (keyword ideas, estimated search volumes, competition and bid ranges) for the operator's own Google Ads account.

What is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the operator's own computer. Keyword statistics are stored in the operator's private research files. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about other people, and it does not use Google user data for advertising.

Sharing

No Google user data is transferred to third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all further data access.
