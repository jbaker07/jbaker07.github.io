Baker Supply keyword research

Baker Supply keyword research is an app operated by Baker Supply Works, a small business in Chicago, Illinois. It is used internally by Baker Supply Works to study how often people search for products and services, so that we can decide which products to source and sell.

What the app does

  • Signs in to the operator's own Google Ads account with Google sign-in (OAuth).
  • Requests keyword ideas for seed words, countries and languages that we choose, using the Google Ads API KeywordPlanIdeaService.GenerateKeywordIdeas method.
  • Requests historical search statistics (average monthly searches, monthly search volumes, competition level and top-of-page bid ranges) for lists of keywords, using KeywordPlanIdeaService.GenerateKeywordHistoricalMetrics.
  • Saves those aggregate keyword statistics to our private research files for analysis.

The app is read-only. It does not create, edit, pause or delete campaigns, ads, keywords, budgets, billing settings or users, and it never spends money on advertising.

What user data the app requests, and why

The app requests one Google permission: the Google Ads API scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). Google requires this scope for all Google Ads API calls, including keyword planning. We use it only to read keyword planning statistics through the operator's own Google Ads account. The statistics are aggregate and anonymous search estimates; they contain no information about individual people.

The app does not read email, contacts, files or any other Google data. The sign-in token is stored only on the operator's own computer. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Who can use it

This is an internal tool. It is not offered to the public; only Baker Supply Works staff sign in.
