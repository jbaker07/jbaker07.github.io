Baker Supply keyword research

Baker Supply keyword research is an internal, read-only tool used by Baker Supply Works (Chicago, Illinois) to study search demand for products and services.

It signs in to the operator's own Google Ads account and uses the Google Ads API Keyword Planning services (keyword ideas and historical search metrics) to collect aggregate keyword statistics. It does not create or change campaigns, budgets, billing or ads, and it is not offered to other users.
